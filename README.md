# EX No.5.1.DESIGN THE 3D MODEL AS PROVIDED AND CONVERT TO ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW
## DATE:11.11.26

## AIM: 
To create orthographic view of given 3D model

## REQUIREMENTS: 
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE
Draw the base plate outline with rounded ends; dimension it to your desired size.

Extrude the outline to form the base, then add four mounting holes with Hole or Extrude Cut.

<img width="463" height="512" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-17 203506" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9bd1e571-bb64-4b6e-9192-7f5dbc85130d" />

Sketch a centered circle on the base and extrude it upward to create the raised cylindrical boss.

<img width="457" height="171" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-17 203728" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1750c79-f7bf-4e76-83cc-68eaa819d9fc" />

Cut a concentric hole through the boss; add a shallow inner step if needed.

Sketch the central raised support shape around the boss and extrude it to the required height.

Add the triangular reinforcing ribs between the support and the base using Rib or sketch-and-extrude.
